Dlo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Overview

Dlo is a local file manager and browser for saving, organizing, previewing, and managing files on your device. Dlo is designed to stay simple, private, and focused on files that users are allowed to save and manage.

The app does not require an account for its core features. Files saved in Dlo remain on the user's device unless the user chooses to share, export, delete, or move them.

Data Collection

Dlo does not collect personal data from the app for tracking, advertising, or resale.

The app may store local browsing data such as bookmarks, history, tabs, file records, folder choices, and download records on the device so the app can provide its features. This information is stored locally and is not sent to Dlo.

Analytics and App Configuration

Dlo uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve the app. This service processes app-instance and device identifiers, app interactions such as app opens and screen views, device and app information, purchase events, and approximate location derived from the network connection. Analytics events can be associated with the app installation even though Dlo does not require an account.

Dlo also uses Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations to retrieve app configuration, including whether the subscription screen is enabled. These services process installation identifiers and technical information needed to deliver configuration. Local files and browsing history are not sent to these services by Dlo.

These services are used for analytics and app functionality, not cross-app advertising tracking. For information about Google's processing, see the Firebase privacy information.

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. Dlo does not receive full payment card details.

Dlo uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status, validate purchases, restore purchases, and analyze subscription activity. RevenueCat processes purchase information associated with an anonymous app user identifier so the app can unlock Pro features and keep subscription access in sync. Firebase Analytics may also record purchase events associated with the app installation. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Diagnostics

Dlo uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and technical diagnostics, including stack traces, device and operating-system information, app state, and activity breadcrumbs provided by Firebase Analytics. These reports help us identify and fix errors. Dlo does not set a personal account identifier in Crashlytics or send local file contents or browsing history in crash reports.

Apple may provide developers with crash logs, diagnostics, and aggregated performance information depending on the user's device settings.

Third-Party Websites

Dlo includes a browser. Websites visited by the user are operated by third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Dlo does not control third-party websites, their content, or their download permissions. Some websites may restrict downloading, saving, offline access, or reuse of their content.

User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the right to save, store, view, or share any file, and for complying with applicable laws, copyright rules, and each third-party website's terms.

Contact
